      How to play

      • Click or drag a piece. Dots show where it can go; rings show captures.
      • On one laptop, the board turns to the side of whoever is to move (setting: Turn the board for each player).
      • Right-click and drag to draw arrows; right-click a square to circle it.
      • Play a colleague: create a game and share its 6-character code once. Moves then appear on both laptops as they are played.
      • Your current game survives a page reload. Finished games go to Saved games.
